Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. When it happens in Melbourne, Melbourne Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Melbourne

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
